# Choose between a proof loop and durable task recovery

Use the free Community edition when your job is to learn and exercise a bounded authorization → observed outcome → evidence-bound claim loop. It exposes seven tools and stores its ledger locally. It is not a durable context-memory service.

Use the [current product matrix](../PRODUCT.md) to compare the licensed Complete Local offer. Its additional jobs include durable context retrieval, recovery checkpoints, Ed25519-signed trace export, release-byte verification, opt-in adoption receipts and multi-agent coordination. The live [pricing page](https://living-stack-mcp.pages.dev/#pricing) is the purchase destination; inspect its current license and fulfillment terms before buying.

## Test the job you actually need

For an evaluation, write down one task you lose time on: recovering a interrupted run, handing work to another agent, or proving which release bytes you tested. Complete the [Community proof loop](first-proof.md) first if you need to understand the evidence model. That proves only its seven-tool scope.

A task that specifically requires durable resume should be evaluated against the licensed runtime's recovery path. Do not count a successful Community session close as proof of paid recovery. Likewise, a signed export binds a signer and bytes; it does not certify every factual claim inside the trace.

## What to report before onboarding

State the host/client, operating system, the step where progress stopped and the next capability needed. Do not send API keys, private paths, proprietary task content or ledger exports. The [pilot feedback form](../PILOT.md) is public. Paid support and download recovery should use the licensed offer's own support route.

This is a product-fit guide, not a claim that the product prevents all hallucinations, creates independent agency, or guarantees savings. An honest failed evaluation is more useful than a passing check for the wrong task.
